# Roadmap

What is built, what is being worked on, and what is planned. Plans can change.

## Done

- Sign in with email, optional Google sign-in, password reset
- Syllabus upload (PDF) or manual topics, with retrieval-grounded questions
- Four interview types and four company styles
- Claim-by-claim fact-checking with confidence, critical errors and score caps
- Adaptive follow-up questions
- Live pacing: timer per answer, soft target, average time in the debrief
- Claims list, session history with replay, progress page, weak topic trends
- Debrief with score ring, topic chart and PDF export
- Study mode: chat with your documents and generate quizzes
- Voice tutor on local speech models with cloud fallback: doubt mode, Hinglish mode, notes and flashcards, roadmaps, walkthroughs, videos, Python code-along
- Per-user rate limits, backend auth, delete-my-data, privacy and terms pages
- Continuous integration, deploy guide

## In progress

- Polishing the first-run experience and mobile layouts

## Planned

- Job description and resume grounding: sessions that combine the syllabus, a role's requirements and the student's own experience
- Gap detection: show what a role needs that the syllabus and resume do not cover
- Deadline-driven prep plan: a day-by-day schedule that works back from a date
- Spaced re-testing of weak topics
- A "why this question" view that shows the source chunks behind each question
- Ask Aria about a scored answer from study mode

## Not planned

- College-specific syllabus presets: the point is to use each student's own syllabus
- Panel interviews and multiple interviewer personalities
